## Friendly proposal to Abby — barter pitch

A short, warm, one-page proposal you can hand to Abby (or email) offering a website-for-materials trade. Tone: neighbor to neighbor, not consultant to client. No jargon, no hard sell.

### Format

Generate as **PDF** (and matching .docx for editing), saved to `/mnt/documents/`:
- `BTY_Website_Trade_Proposal_for_Abby.pdf`
- `BTY_Website_Trade_Proposal_for_Abby.docx`

Visual style matches the prior two packets (same fonts, same restrained layout) so it reads as part of the same set.

### What goes in it

**1. Opener — who I am, why I'm asking**
- Live in Oakham, a few towns over
- Bought from BTY before, loved the woman-owned, family-run story
- Work in software / web — building sites is what I do

**2. What I noticed about the current site**
- Said kindly: the current site is hard to use on a phone, slow, and buries the things customers actually want (phone number, hours, prices, quote form)
- Frame as "leaving money on the table," not "your site is bad"

**3. What I've already built**
- A complete replacement is live at buytheyard.lovable.app — already has the catalog, prices, delivery info, hours, quote form, contact, about page
- Built to her actual business: phone-first, posted prices, Central Mass delivery, seasonal hours, the 4% card fee explained honestly
- She can click around before deciding anything

**4. The trade**
- I finish the migration (DNS, email kept intact, Wix cancelled) and hand her the keys
- She owns the site, can edit it herself or have me maintain it
- In exchange: landscaping materials for my Oakham project — a berm plus mulch and stone for the yard
- Rough scope of what I need (yards of loam for the berm, mulch yards, stone yards) so she can ballpark the value on her end
- Note that I'm open to her counter — if the material value doesn't match the work, we figure out the difference

**5. Why this works for both of us**
- For her: a real website, no out-of-pocket cost, no monthly Wix fee, faster phone calls and quote requests, better Google ranking, kept Google Workspace email
- For me: materials I'm going to buy anyway, from a neighbor I'd rather support than a big-box yard
- Local, neighborly, no middlemen

**6. What happens if she says yes**
- One short call or yard visit to confirm the trade
- She gives me the access listed in the companion packet (Network Solutions, Google Workspace, Wix)
- I do the migration in a single afternoon
- Materials get scheduled whenever convenient for her crew

**7. Closing**
- No pressure, no expiration — offer stands either way
- My phone, my email, and the preview URL
